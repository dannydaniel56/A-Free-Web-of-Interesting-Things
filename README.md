## Learning & Knowledge

### Global Platforms (Work Anywhere)

- **[Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/)** - Completely free, no ads. Math, science, economics, and more. Available worldwide
- **[Coursera](https://www.coursera.org/)** - University courses from Yale, Stanford, and Google. Free to audit, pay only for certificates
- **[edX](https://www.edx.org/)** - Courses from Harvard, MIT, and 250+ universities. Free to learn, certificate optional
- **[MIT OpenCourseWare](https://ocw.mit.edu/)** - Real MIT course materials: problem sets, exams, lectures. 2,500+ courses, completely free
- **[OpenLearn](https://www.open.edu/openlearn/)** - The Open University's free courses. 900+ with digital badges, no entry requirements
- **[OpenStax](https://openstax.org/)** - Free, peer-reviewed textbooks for university courses
- **[Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/)** - 60,000+ classic books and references, instant download
- **[CK-12](https://www.ck12.org/)** - Free STEM textbooks with animations and interactive activities
- **[Class Central](https://www.class-central.com/)** - Search and review platform. Indexes major MOOC platforms globally
- **[Wikiversity](https://www.wikiversity.org/)** - Wikimedia's platform for free educational materials and online courses
